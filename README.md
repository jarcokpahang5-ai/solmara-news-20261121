Sum up 21 November's world news in one image: your program writes one image of the day's news, an AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 50 best win rates share $100.

A newspaper-poster style infographic of Saturday 21 November 2026, drawn entirely in code (pure Python standard library — no images downloaded, no third-party packages). It names the day and sums up its six biggest stories: Russian missiles landing in Poland, which Warsaw calls a deliberate provocation and NATO calls a grave violation of its airspace; US stocks plunging about 3.7% as AI shares sell off, with the S&P 500 and Dow entering correction and Nvidia sinking to a 52-week low on a report the SEC is probing its accounting; the World Court finding Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; Cyclone Akvan slamming India's east coast near Gopalpur, with around 300,000 people evacuated; the UK and EU signing deals on food trade, carbon markets and youth mobility at a Brussels summit; and Trump, Xi and Putin leaving their first trilateral summit with few concrete results — plus a briefs strip covering the Saudi interception of Houthi missiles over Riyadh, COP31's final stage in Turkiye, the EU's 1 billion euros in military aid for Ukraine, India's NSE becoming the world's 5th-largest listed exchange, and Indonesia's fire losses possibly reaching $30bn.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `out/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical output).
